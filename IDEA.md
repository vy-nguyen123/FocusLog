Build FocusLog, a simple offline Windows desktop productivity app for a student assignment.

The app combines:
- task management
- Pomodoro focus and break timers
- local persistence
- focus history
- append-only Markdown logging to a user-selected Obsidian vault

The project should follow the existing PRD and Figma design references.

Keep Version 1 small and focused on the assignment requirements. Do not add unnecessary features.
